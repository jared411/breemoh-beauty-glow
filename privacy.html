Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. What we collect

When you book a service, order a product or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, email address (if you give it), appointment or delivery details, and any notes you add. For treatments, we may also keep health information you choose to share, such as allergies or skin conditions, so we can treat you safely.

2. Why we collect it

We use your information to book and deliver your service or order, reply to you, take payment, keep the records that the law requires, and keep you safe during treatments. We do not sell your information.

3. Who we share it with

We share your details only with people who help us run the business, such as our messaging and hosting providers and delivery riders, and only as far as needed. We may also share them if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep booking and order records for as long as needed for accounting, and treatment notes for as long as the law or safe practice requires. We delete other information when it is no longer needed.

5. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. Contact us on WhatsApp or at breemohbeautyglow@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website is completely secure, so please share only what is needed for your booking or order.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. Your shopping cart, if you have one, is saved on your own device.
