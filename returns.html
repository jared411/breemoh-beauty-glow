Refunds and Cancellations

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Your rights first

Nothing on this page takes away your rights under Kenya's Consumer Protection Act, 2012. If a service or product is faulty or not as described, we will put it right or refund you.

Cancelling or moving an appointment

Please tell us at least 24 hours before your appointment, on WhatsApp, if you need to cancel or move it. We will then move it or refund any deposit you paid. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed appointments, may be charged in full.

Deposits

For some services we may ask for a deposit to hold your slot. The deposit is part of the price. It is refunded if we cancel, or if you cancel with 24 hours' notice.

Refunds

Refunds are paid back to the method you used to pay, within 7 working days after we confirm the cancellation or receive the item back.

Products

If a product arrives damaged, faulty or is not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos, and we will replace it or refund you. Products that have been opened or used cannot be returned, for hygiene reasons, unless they are faulty.

How to start

Send us a message on WhatsApp at +254 740 494 230 with your name, booking or order details, and photos where relevant. We reply within one working day.
